Common Questions

Frequently asked

Is the trial class free? +

Yes — every new student gets one free trial class before enrolling, across any of our three instruments.

What age can my child start? +

Piano and Keyboard classes are open from age 6, and Guitar from age 8, though we assess each child individually.

Do you offer 1-on-1 lessons? +

Yes, both small-group and one-on-one formats are available depending on the batch and instrument.

Do I need to own an instrument to start? +

Not for your first few classes — we'll guide you on what to buy once you're ready to practice at home.
